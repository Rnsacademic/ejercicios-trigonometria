1. TV Shows

News: noticias.

Game show: concurso de preguntas o retos.

Sitcom: serie de comedia sobre situaciones cotidianas.

Documentary: programa informativo sobre hechos reales.

Talent show: competencia de canto, baile u otros talentos.

Cartoon: programa animado.

Reality show: programa con personas reales en situaciones no guionadas.

Weather report: reporte del clima.

Sports show: programa deportivo.

Soap opera: serie dramática sobre relaciones y problemas familiares.

2. Simple Present

I / You / We / They → verbo en forma base.

Example: They watch TV on Fridays.

He / She / It → normalmente se agrega -s o -es.

Example: She watches the news.

3. Negative Form

I / You / We / They → don't + verb

They don't watch reality shows.

He / She / It → doesn't + verb

He doesn't like cartoons.

Después de doesn't, el verbo vuelve a su forma base: doesn't like, no doesn't likes.

4. Questions with DO / DOES

Do + I / you / we / they + verb...?

Do you like documentaries?

Does + he / she / it + verb...?

Does Maria watch the news?

Puntaje: 0 / 20

Respuestas cortas

Answer each question in one complete sentence.

1. What kind of TV shows do you like and why?

2. What is your favorite TV show?

3. How often do you watch TV?

4. Do you watch TV with your family?

5. What TV shows does your family like?

6. Do you like documentaries? Why or why not?

7. What time do you usually watch TV?

8. Does your best friend like the same TV shows as you?

9. What TV show do you recommend to a classmate?

10. Why do you think TV shows can be educational?

Suggested answers:
  1. I like documentaries because I learn new things about animals and nature.
  2. My favorite TV show is Planet Adventures.
  3. I watch TV three or four times a week.
  4. Yes, I do. I usually watch TV with my family at night.
  5. My family likes comedies, news programs and sports shows.
  6. Yes, I do because documentaries are interesting and educational.
  7. I usually watch TV at 7:00 p.m.
  8. Yes, he/she does. We both like comedy shows.
  9. I recommend Planet Adventures because it is interesting and educational.
  10. TV shows can be educational because they can teach us about science, history, nature and other cultures.

These are examples. Other complete and grammatically correct answers are also valid.

Desarrollo

Write 6 to 8 sentences about your favorite TV show or movie. Include the name, type, characters, when you watch it, why you like it and your personal opinion. Use the Simple Present.

Rúbrica sugerida (10 puntos)
  • Contenido completo: 4 pts
  • Uso correcto de Simple Present: 3 pts
  • Vocabulario del tema: 2 pts
  • Mayúsculas y puntuación: 1 pt
My favorite TV show is Planet Adventures. It is a documentary about nature. The program shows animals from different countries. I watch it on Saturday evenings. My family sometimes watches it with me. I like the show because it is interesting and educational. It teaches me new facts about the planet. I think it is an excellent TV show.
